What was achieved

Strategic Insights

Key Project Takeaways

A primary discovery during this research was the effectiveness of Selective Validation. We proved that by filtering for high-confidence anomalies at the AI layer, we could reduce Blockchain storage overhead by nearly 90% without sacrificing system transparency.

Furthermore, the fusion of Convolutional (Spatial) and Recurrent (Temporal) layers proved vital; neither architecture alone could reach the accuracy levels required for modern smart grid stability.
