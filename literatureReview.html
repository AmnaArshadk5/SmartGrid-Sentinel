Research Streams

Foundational Paradigms

AI-Based Detection 🤖

Utilizes Deep Learning architectures (CNN/LSTM) to map spatial correlations and temporal dependencies. These models excel at recognizing patterns invisible to human operators.

Critical Limitation

Centralized dependency creates single points of failure and lacks immutable audit logs for forensic study.

Blockchain Security 🔗

Decentralizes trust using distributed ledgers. While excellent for data integrity, traditional consensus mechanisms struggle with the velocity of grid telemetry.

Critical Limitation

The "Latency Wall": PoW/PoS consensus delays are incompatible with sub-millisecond fault isolation requirements.

Hybrid Frameworks ⚡

Integrates AI detection with Blockchain logging. This seeks to balance the predictive power of neural networks with the security of a ledger.

Critical Limitation

Inefficient filtering leads to storage bottlenecks or significant synchronization lags across the network nodes.

The Identified Research Gap

A fundamental tension exists in current literature: the higher the security (Blockchain), the lower the real-time responsiveness. Conversely, high-speed AI systems remain vulnerable to data poisoning. This work fills that void by introducing the Proof of Monitoring (PoM) consensus, allowing high-fidelity CNN-LSTM detections to be verified at speeds previously thought impossible in decentralized systems.
