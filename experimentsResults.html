Key findings

Key insights from results

Spatial + temporal fusion winsCNN-LSTM (98.15%) significantly outperforms CNN-only (93.1%) and LSTM-only (94.4%), confirming that both spatial and temporal modeling are essential.
PoM adds security at zero latency costAdding the PoM layer raised accuracy slightly to 98.2% and added tamper-proof logging with no measurable latency increase.
145× faster than standard blockchainStandard blockchain: 145.2 ms. Proposed PoM hybrid: 0.82 ms. The selective validation strategy eliminates 99% of unnecessary consensus operations.
Reaction time (τ) is the top featureFeature importance analysis reveals τ and power imbalance (p) are the strongest predictors of grid instability - aligning with domain knowledge.
Near-perfect class separationAUC of 0.9981 and bimodal prediction confidence distribution confirm the model rarely confuses stable and unstable grid states.
Optimal PoM threshold at 0.95Threshold analysis shows 0.95 provides the best trade-off: minimal false blockchain writes while catching all genuine high-confidence faults.
